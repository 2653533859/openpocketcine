Since open beta build 128

New and changed

- Camera setup, settings, View Assist and Media controls are closer to the other OpenCine apps, with the same look and behavior.
- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips, Select all and transfer progress stay responsive while clips cache.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting, because the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tapping the storage readout steps through free space, free percentage and remaining time, with the same text in portrait and landscape.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. In portrait, readouts use a three-column grid that grows with the rows.
- Media scrolls to the newest clip. Offline, it says the camera is disconnected and shows the clips cached on this phone.
- The ISO drum stars the native ISO of the current colour profile, for example 400 in D-Log M.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- The app no longer freezes or shows App not responding while the live picture rebuilds, for example after rotating or returning to the app.
- Reconnecting waits while the app is in the background and resumes when you return, so the live picture comes back without Retry.
- The reconnect card names its 3-minute window. When the link drops, the signal shows no bars and TalkBack says the live link is down.
- Returning from Media, even after a long visit, restores the live picture instead of disconnecting the camera.
- Connection failures open the relevant Wi-Fi settings, and pairing timeouts explain what to try next.
- Shutter Angle follows angle picks, Auto-to-Manual and frame-rate changes again, and the shutter list is no longer stuck at 1/60.
- Osmo Pocket 3, Pocket 4, Pocket 4 Pro, Nano and Action 6 offer the Auto ISO ceiling, the ISO limit and the native ISO hop again.
- On a Pocket 3 that reports no shutter list, Speed offers the standard shutter speeds and sets them.
- The live top bar readouts get their dark glow back, so they stay readable over bright pictures.
- Playback keeps Pause through buffering, scrubbing and Share.
- Playback stays smoother on large cards; scopes and audio meters update without redrawing the whole player.
- Clip deletion, caching and sharing several cached clips work together on the current selection. The selection bar shows the whole count.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip finishes downloading after you close it and reads CACHED. TalkBack reads that setting as one switch.
- Shared clips keep their DJI file names instead of arriving with long random names.
- The open landscape View Assist palette hides the camera values it covers. Portrait card-space labels keep their full value near the cutout.

What to test

- Open Media on a full card. Scroll, select clips, use Select all and cache a few. Check the screen stays responsive and progress keeps moving.
- Rotate the phone repeatedly and leave and return to the app during Live View. Check the app stays responsive and the live picture returns.
- In Live View, go Home, record 60 to 90 s with the system camera app, then return: the picture comes back by itself with no Retry.
- At 180 degrees/25p in Auto, check native ISO and Auto ISO limits. Select Manual, then 50p: 180 stays selected as 1/50 becomes 1/100.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip briefly, go back and wait: it reads CACHED. Play with scopes or audio meters, then Share two clips and check their names.
