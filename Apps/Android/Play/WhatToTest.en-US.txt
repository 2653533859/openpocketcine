New and changed

- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips, Select all and transfer progress stay responsive while clips cache.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting, because the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tapping the storage readout steps through free space, free percentage and remaining time, with the same text in portrait and landscape.

Fixes

- Reconnecting waits while the app is in the background and resumes when you return, so the live picture comes back without Retry. The card names its 3 minute window.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip finishes downloading after you close it and reads CACHED. TalkBack reads that setting as one switch.
- Shared clips keep their DJI file names. The selection bar shows the whole count, and offline Media says the camera is disconnected.
- The open landscape View Assist palette hides the camera values it covers instead of showing them around its edges.

What to test

- Open Media on a full card. Scroll, select clips, use Select all and cache a few. Check the screen stays responsive and progress keeps moving.
- In Live View, go Home, record 60 to 90 s with the system camera app, then return: the picture comes back by itself with no Retry.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip a few seconds, go back and wait about 30 s: it reads CACHED. Share two cached clips and check their names.
