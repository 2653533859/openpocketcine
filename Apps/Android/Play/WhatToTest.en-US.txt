New and changed

- The ISO drum stars the native ISO of the current color profile (for example 400 in D-Log M).
- The live top bar readouts get their dark glow back, so they stay readable over bright pictures.

Fixes

- Osmo Pocket and Action cameras offer Auto ISO ceiling, the ISO limit and the native ISO hop again.
- After a long Media visit, the live picture comes back instead of the camera disconnecting.

What to test

- Open the ISO drum in D-Log M and in D-Log2 and check that the native ISO carries a star.
- Set ISO to Auto and check that the ISO ceiling and native ISO options are offered.
- Stay in Media for 30 minutes or more and return to Live View; the picture should come back.
