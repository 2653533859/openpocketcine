New and changed

- Exposure, shutter angle and frame-rate readouts follow the values supplied by your connected camera.
- Storage readouts preserve the camera's reported values; missing readings are marked unavailable.
- Recording duration follows confirmed camera recording state.

Fixes

- Fixed ML Kit initialization in minified Android releases to prevent a crash when live video starts.
- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; pressing it prevents an unexpected restart.
- Playback stays paused when a late loop, seek or scrub finishes. Press Play to resume.
- ISO controls follow the camera's declared settings, including its native ISO choices.
- Camera controls and Media use the connected camera's available settings.

What to test

- Connect your camera, try manual and automatic exposure, and compare ISO, shutter and storage readouts with the camera screen.
- Start and stop a short recording on the camera. Check the duration and return from Media to Live View.
- Reconnect and rotate the phone. Check missing readings and confirm that the controls stay usable.
- Connect your camera, keep live view running and try face tracking. Report any crash after the first picture.
- Open Media and try short and long clips, switch clips, pause while buffering, then scrub or return to Media. Playback should restart only when you press Play.
- If a clip will not open or live view drops out, share diagnostics and include its duration, whether it was cached and whether camera controls still responded.
