New and changed

- Exposure, shutter angle and frame-rate readouts follow the values supplied by your connected camera.
- Storage readouts preserve the camera's reported values; missing readings are marked unavailable.
- Recording duration follows confirmed camera recording state.

Fixes

- Fixed ML Kit initialization in minified Android releases to prevent a crash when live video starts.
- Pause remains available while playback waits for data; using it prevents unexpected restarts. Late loops, seeks and scrubs stay paused until you press Play.
- ISO controls follow the camera's declared settings, including its native ISO choices.
- Camera controls and Media use the connected camera's available settings.

What to test

- Connect your camera, keep live view running and try face tracking. Test manual and automatic exposure, and compare ISO, shutter and storage readouts with the camera screen.
- Start and stop a short recording on the camera and check its duration. In Media, try short and long clips, pause while buffering, scrub and switch clips, then return to Live View. Playback should restart only when you press Play.
- Reconnect and rotate the phone; check missing readings and usable controls. Report any crash after the first picture. If a clip will not open or live view drops out, share diagnostics with its duration, whether it was cached and whether controls still responded.
