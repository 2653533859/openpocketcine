New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- The app no longer freezes or shows App not responding while the live picture rebuilds, for example after rotating or returning to the app.
- Shutter Angle follows exposure and frame-rate changes. The ISO drum marks native ISO and restores supported Auto ISO limits and native ISO choices.
- Returning from a long Media visit restores the live picture. Playback keeps Pause through buffering, scrubbing and Share.
- Clip deletion, caching and sharing work together. Connection failures offer the relevant Wi-Fi settings and clearer pairing-timeout guidance.

What to test

- Rotate the phone repeatedly and leave and return to the app during Live View. Check the app stays responsive and the live picture returns.
- Select 180 degrees at 25p, switch Auto to Manual, then 50p. Keep 180 selected as shutter time changes from 1/50 to 1/100; check native ISO limits.
- In Media, pause, scrub and share a clip, then return to Live View. If the picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time.
