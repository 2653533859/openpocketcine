New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- Shutter Angle follows exposure and frame-rate changes. The ISO drum marks native ISO and restores supported Auto ISO limits and native ISO choices.
- Returning from a long Media visit restores the live picture. Playback keeps Pause through buffering and scrubbing.
- Camera-clip deletion, full-resolution caching and sharing several cached clips work together, with clearer camera-playback messages.
- Connection failures offer the relevant Wi-Fi settings and clearer pairing-timeout guidance. Top-bar readouts stay readable over bright pictures.

What to test

- Select 180 degrees at 25p, switch Auto to Manual, then 50p. Keep 180 selected as shutter time changes from 1/50 to 1/100; check native ISO, ISO limits and portrait/landscape readouts.
- In Media, pause and scrub a clip, switch clips, cancel a deletion and share cached clips. Return to Live View and check that the live picture resumes.
- If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time, what you were doing and whether camera controls still responded.
