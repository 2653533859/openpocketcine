New and changed

- Share several cached clips at once from Media; they reach the share sheet together.

Fixes

- Media shows its notes again, including when the camera's own playback screen is open.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, playing a clip from the connected camera caches its original again.
- After a long Media visit, the live picture comes back instead of the camera disconnecting.

What to test

- Cache two or more clips, select them in Media and share. Check that all of them reach the app you pick.
- Open playback on the camera itself, then open Media in the app and check the note. Close camera playback and refresh.
- Turn on Full Resolution Caching, play a clip that is not cached yet and check that it shows as cached afterwards.
