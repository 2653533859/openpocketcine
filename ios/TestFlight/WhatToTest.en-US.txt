New and changed

- Media shows a short note when the camera's own playback screen is open, instead of an empty gallery.

Fixes

- If the camera does not enter playback, Media now says so instead of naming the clip list.
- With filters on and the camera disconnected, an empty gallery names the filters instead of the cache.
- In portrait, the large format readout lines up exactly with the clock at the top of the screen.

What to test

- Open playback on the camera itself, then open Media in the app. Check the note, close camera playback and refresh.
- Disconnect, open Media, turn on a filter that matches nothing and check the message names the filters.
- Use a volume button as the remote shutter to start and stop a recording; report any unexpected quit.
