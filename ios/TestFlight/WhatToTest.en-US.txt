New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- Shutter Angle follows exposure changes and frame rate again, and the ISO controls restore the camera's supported native ISO and Auto ISO limits.
- The app no longer hangs while capture readouts update during a long session. VoiceOver no longer reaches live controls under a covering screen.
- Returning from Media restores the live picture. Playback keeps Pause through buffering, seeking, Share and returning to the app.
- Media deletion, caching and sharing stay tied to the current clips. Refused camera Wi-Fi joins show guidance and a way to open Wi-Fi settings.

What to test

- At 180 degrees/25p, check native ISO and Auto ISO limits in Auto. Select Manual, then 50p: 180 stays selected as 1/50 becomes 1/100.
- In Media, play a clip and tap Share. Check it pauses while the share sheet is up and stays paused after you close it. Then check Live View resumes.
- Keep a long session running with capture readouts open. If controls stall or the picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time.
