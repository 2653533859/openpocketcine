New and changed

- A more consistent OpenCine interface brings camera setup, settings, View Assist and media controls closer together across the apps.
- In landscape, the colour profile sits beside the codec. Portrait readouts keep their rounded card-space values clear of the screen cutout.
- Connection diagnostics separate incoming video, decoding and display activity to help investigate live-picture interruptions.

Fixes

- Shutter Angle follows exposure changes and frame rate again, and the ISO controls restore the camera's supported native ISO and Auto ISO limits.
- Returning from a long Media visit restores the live picture. Playback keeps Pause through buffering, seeking and returning to the app.
- Media selection, deletion, full-resolution caching and sharing stay tied to the current clips, with clearer camera-playback and filter messages.
- Refused camera Wi-Fi joins show clear guidance and a way to open Wi-Fi settings. A mismatched watcher is asked to update the app.

What to test

- At 180 degrees/25p, check supported native ISO and Auto ISO limits in Auto. Select Manual, then 50p: 180 stays selected as 1/50 becomes 1/100. Rotate; codec, colour and card space stay readable.
- In Media, pause and scrub a clip, switch clips, cancel a deletion and share cached clips. Return to Live View and check that the live picture resumes.
- If the live picture drops out, use Share Diagnostics and report the time, what you were doing and whether camera controls still responded.
