New and changed

- Media is much faster on large cards: listing, selecting clips, Select all and transfer progress stay responsive while clips cache.
- The live picture runs more smoothly after connecting, because the screen no longer redraws everything as the timecode advances.
- Tapping the storage readout steps through free space, free percentage and remaining time, with the same text in portrait and landscape.

Fixes

- An approved camera Wi-Fi join is kept for rejoins, and a retry no longer asks to join again. Forgetting a camera setup also removes its Wi-Fi network.
- The Local Network prompt now appears during pairing, before the camera Wi-Fi join, and Allow carries on pairing without relaunching the app.
- Reconnecting waits while the app is in the background, so the live picture can return without Retry. The card names its 3 minute reconnect window.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, a played clip finishes downloading after you close it and reads CACHED. The open landscape View Assist palette hides the values it covers.

What to test

- Open Media on a full card. Scroll, select clips, use Select all and cache a few. Check the screen stays responsive and progress keeps moving.
- Delete and reinstall, then pair: the Local Network prompt comes before the Wi-Fi Join alert. Later go Home for over 3 minutes and return: live view comes back without Retry.
- With Full Resolution Caching on, play an uncached clip a few seconds, go back and wait about 30 s: it reads CACHED. Tap the storage readout in portrait and landscape.
