New and changed

- Media shows a short note when the camera's own playback screen is open, instead of an empty gallery.
- In portrait, the large format readout now lines up exactly with the clock at the top of the screen.

Fixes

- With Shutter Angle on, picking an angle now sets the shutter, and the shutter list is no longer stuck at 1/60.
- Media names the real cause of an empty gallery: a camera that did not enter playback, or filters while disconnected.
- After the landscape assist palette collapses, its hidden buttons are no longer reachable with VoiceOver, and a volume-button remote shutter is protected against a rare quit.
- After a long Media visit, the live picture comes back instead of the camera disconnecting.

What to test

- Turn on Shutter Angle, pick 180 degrees at 25p, then switch EXPOSURE to Auto and back to Manual; check the shutter follows and other speeds are offered.
- Open playback on the camera, then Media in the app, and check the note. Disconnected, a filter that matches nothing should name the filters.
- Use a volume button as the remote shutter to start and stop a recording, stay in Media for 30 minutes and return; the live picture should come back.
