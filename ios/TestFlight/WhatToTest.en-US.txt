New and changed

- In landscape, the colour profile now appears beside the codec in the top bar.

Fixes

- Portrait card-space labels keep their full rounded value near the screen cutout.
- A refused camera Wi-Fi join now gives clearer guidance and a way to open Wi-Fi settings.
- Media playback and deletion stay tied to the current clip after switching pages.

What to test

- Rotate between portrait and landscape while connected. Check the codec, colour profile and free card space stay readable.
- Open Media, change the selected clip and cancel a deletion. Check the selection and playback still match.
- Refuse a camera Wi-Fi join, follow the Wi-Fi settings guidance and retry.
