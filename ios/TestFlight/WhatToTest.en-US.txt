New and changed

- Osmo Pocket and Action cameras offer Auto ISO ceiling, the ISO limit and the native ISO hop again.
- In portrait, the large format readout now lines up exactly with the clock at the top of the screen.

Fixes

- On a Pocket 3 that reports no shutter list, Speed offers the standard shutter speeds and sets them.
- With Shutter Angle on, picking an angle now sets the shutter, and the shutter list is no longer stuck at 1/60.
- After a long Media visit, the live picture comes back instead of the camera disconnecting.

What to test

- On a Pocket 3 in manual exposure, open Speed, pick a few shutter speeds and confirm the camera follows.
- Set ISO to Auto and check that the ISO ceiling and native ISO options are offered.
- Stay in Media for 30 minutes or more and return; the live picture should come back.
